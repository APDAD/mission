What makes practices adopt, stay, or leave a supplement partner

Ten benchmarks from the practitioner-channel majors and top-decile rep research, each translated to your assets: NDCP storefronts, autoship, the handouts machine, seminars/CE, the 351-account census, the same-day reply machine. Vendor-reported numbers are labeled; trust direction over magnitude. Full sourced file: nutridyn-territory/playbooks/world-benchmarks.md

1. Refills are engineered, not hoped for

THE BEST Fullscript reports autoship patients are 70% more likely to refill and app use lifts adherence ~40% (vendor-reported).

FOR YOU Autoship enrollment is the annuity behind every storefront. Every handout and day-7 follow-up closes on "set it to autoship," not just "place your order."

fullscript.com

2. The ordering click lives inside the doctor's workflow

THE BEST Practices that wire the dispensary into their EHR see ~16% more monthly revenue and 33% more recommendations fulfilled (vendor-reported).

FOR YOU You cannot build EHR integrations, but you can put the storefront link and QR inside every handout, deck, and front desk. One click between recommendation and order.

fullscript.com

3. In-office inventory is the adoption killer

THE BEST Inventory is the most time-consuming and expensive part of in-office dispensing; one practitioner-author puts walk-back-in repurchase at 5-10% (single-author estimate).

FOR YOU Lead every new-account pitch with: zero inventory risk, refills happen from home, margin still codes to you. The storefront answers the two frictions that stop adoption cold.

todayspractitioner.com

4. Education is the lock-in mechanism

THE BEST Standard Process runs a CE symposium (up to 12 credits) and gates account perks through education; Thorne and DFH run standing webinar series.

FOR YOU Aug 29 and the FN cert sponsorship ARE the retention machine. A doctor who earned CE through you re-buys through you. Make a CE-bearing event an annual rhythm.

chiroeco.com, nutraingredients.com

5. Certify the staff, because the staff place the reorders

THE BEST Metagenics FirstLine Therapy certifies the doctor AND "Lifestyle Educators" plus program coordinators, with implementation support after.

FOR YOU Build a 30-minute front-desk track for every top-25 account: run the storefront, answer the price question, hand the handout. The doctor picks the brand; the staff keep it moving.

newhope.com

6. Channel integrity is the price defense

THE BEST Thorne restricted Amazon to one authorized storefront and serialized every label against diversion; the whole channel enforces MAP because channel bleed is the top-named threat.

FOR YOU The Amazon objection has a scripted answer: gray-market listings carry diversion and storage risk, and the patient storefront prices comparably while the margin codes to the practice. Put it in the staff track and handouts.

thorne.com, supplysidesj.com

7. Adoption is won on verifiable science

THE BEST Practitioners adopt brands showing sourcing, QC proof, meaningful dosing and human trials; physicians rank reps with clinical studies and follow-through highest.

FOR YOU Your citation-law blasts and annotated study pages are exactly the top-of-market behavior. Keep every product rec chained to a real paper. Most territory reps never build this habit.

nutraingredients.com, reutersevents.com

8. Access is rationed; win it asynchronously

THE BEST 53% of US physicians restrict rep visits, up from 23% in 2008. They prefer concise evidence-based updates on their own clock.

FOR YOU The Monday blast plus same-day replies is the correct architecture. The drop-in is the garnish; the asynchronous evidence-led touch is the meal. Protect the once-a-week ceiling.

prnewswire.com

9. Top-decile reps run business reviews, not check-ins

THE BEST High performers run a layered cadence ending in quarterly business reviews where every meeting produces decisions with owners and dates; even top reps need ~5 touches to convert.

FOR YOU The 351-account census is a QBR engine nobody else in the territory has. Quarterly, top 25: one page of what their patients bought, the gap, one protocol to add. A rep who shows a practice its own numbers is irreplaceable.

ziellab.com, highspot.com

10. The channel rewards whoever owns the institutions

THE BEST Practitioner channel share grows 9.2% (2024) to a projected 9.7% (2028); majors anchor to Mayo, IFM, and the colleges, and Standard Process captures practitioners as students.

FOR YOU Life University and the IFM/A4M list are the same institutional-anchor motion the majors run. Students captured on campus become coded accounts for a career; keep the STT tab warm.

nutraingredients.com, standardprocess.com

What could not be verified

No public data was found on why practices DROP partners (backorder rates, rep churn effects); treat those as hypotheses from your own book. All Fullscript percentages are vendor marketing claims. The 5-10% repurchase figure is one practitioner's estimate.
