Fatty Liver Disease
MASLD, the condition formerly called NAFLD

Fatty liver disease is extra fat stored inside liver cells that is not caused by heavy drinking. In 2023, liver specialists renamed it MASLD (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease) because it runs on the same metabolism as insulin resistance, belly fat, high triglycerides and high blood pressure. It is the most common liver condition in the world, affecting roughly 1 in 3 adults, and most people feel nothing. Caught early, it is often reversible.

Lifestyle Recommendations

Eat Mediterranean

Vegetables, lean protein, olive oil, nuts and fish. Cut sugary drinks, juice and refined carbs: they turn into liver fat fastest.

Lose 7 to 10 percent

Losing 7 to 10 percent of body weight is the change most tied to clearing liver fat and calming inflammation.

Move every day

150 minutes a week of brisk walking plus 2 strength days. A 10-minute walk after meals blunts the sugar spike.

Go easy on alcohol

Even though alcohol did not cause it, alcohol adds to the liver's load. Keep it minimal or skip it.
