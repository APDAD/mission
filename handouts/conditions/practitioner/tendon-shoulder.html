Shoulder and Tendon Pain
Rotator cuff, tennis elbow and Achilles

Tendons are tough cords of collagen that join muscle to bone. Most tendon pain builds slowly, when a tendon does more than it is ready for and its rope-like fibers fray. Shoulder pain is the third most common muscle and joint reason to see a family doctor, usually from the rotator cuff: 4 muscles whose tendons hug the ball of the arm bone and keep it in its socket. The supraspinatus helps lift your arm. The other three turn it in and out.

Where tendon pain shows upwhere it usually hurts

Shoulder rotator cuff

  • Ache on the outer upper arm
  • Worse when you lift your arm
  • Hurts to lie on it at night

Elbow tennis elbow

  • Pain on the outer elbow bump
  • Worse when you grip or twist
  • Common with gripping work

Ankle Achilles tendon

  • Pain just above the heel bone
  • Stiff in the morning
  • Sore with running or jumping

12weeks is a typical length for a tendon exercise plan. Tendons rebuild slowly, and the gains last.

Get checked right away for: a pop or sudden weakness after a fall or heavy lift. An arm you cannot lift. A hot, red, swollen joint, or a fever. Numbness running down the arm. A pop at the back of the ankle, or not being able to stand on tiptoe on that foot. Shoulder or arm pain with chest pressure, shortness of breath or a cold sweat can be a heart attack: call 911.

Lifestyle RecommendationsHabits that help tendons rebuild

Load, don’t rest

Exercise most days. Full rest weakens tendons.

Build up slowly

Add weight, miles or reps in small steps.

Lower your reach

Keep heavy lifting below shoulder height.

Feed the rebuild

Eat protein and foods rich in vitamin C.

Sleep 7 to 9 hours

Your repair hormone peaks in deep sleep.

Stop smoking

Smoking is tied to more rotator cuff tears.

Provider checkKeep taking your medicines.

  • Blood thinner, aspirin, ibuprofen, naproxen, or surgery soon? Fish oil and the Inflam-Eze Plus herbs can add to bleeding; many surgeons stop them 2 weeks before. D3 5000 with K2’s vitamin K affects warfarin. Ask your provider first.
  • Fluoroquinolone antibiotics (ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin) can injure tendons, more so after 60 or on steroids. Stop and call your doctor if a tendon hurts.
  • Allergic to aspirin, red meat (alpha-gal) or fish? Skip Inflam-Eze Plus (willow, aspirin’s cousin), Collagen Renew (beef) or Omega Pure (fish oil, gelatin shell).
  • Diabetes medicine? Ask your provider: Inflam-Eze Plus may lower blood sugar. Yellow skin or eyes, or dark urine? Stop Inflam-Eze Plus and call your doctor.
  • Under 18, pregnant or nursing? On sleep aids or other medicines? Stomach ulcer, gallstones, or liver or kidney disease? Ask your provider first.
