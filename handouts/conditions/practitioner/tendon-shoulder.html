Shoulder and Tendon Pain
Rotator cuff, tennis elbow and Achilles

Tendons are tough cords of collagen that join muscle to bone. Most tendon pain is not one big injury: it builds when a tendon does more than it is ready for, and its rope-like fibers fray. Shoulder pain is the third most common muscle and joint reason to see a family doctor, usually from the rotator cuff. The good news: tendons rebuild with the right load.

Where tendon pain shows upwhere it usually hurts

Shoulder rotator cuff

  • Ache on the outer upper arm
  • Worse when you lift your arm
  • Hurts to lie on it at night

Elbow tennis elbow

  • Pain on the outer elbow bump
  • Worse when you grip or twist
  • Common with gripping work

Heel Achilles tendon

  • Pain just above the heel bone
  • Stiff with your first steps
  • Sore with running or jumping

12weeks is a typical length for a tendon exercise plan. Tendons rebuild slowly, and the gains last.

Get checked right away for a sudden pop or weakness after a fall or heavy lift, an arm you cannot lift, a hot, red, swollen joint or a fever, or numbness running down the arm. Shoulder or arm pain with chest pressure, shortness of breath or a cold sweat can be a heart attack: call 911.

Lifestyle RecommendationsHabits that help tendons rebuild

Load, don't rest

Exercise most days. Full rest weakens tendons.

Build up slowly

Add weight, miles or reps in small steps.

Lower your reach

Keep heavy lifting below shoulder height.

Feed the rebuild

Eat protein and vitamin C foods like citrus.

Sleep 7 to 9 hours

Growth hormone peaks in deep sleep.

Stop smoking

Smoking is tied to more rotator cuff tears.

Provider check

  • Fluoroquinolone antibiotics (ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin) can injure tendons, more so after 60 or with steroids. Tendon pain on one? Call your prescriber.
