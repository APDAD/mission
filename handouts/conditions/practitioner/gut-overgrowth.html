Gut Overgrowth
Yeast, parasites and SIBO

Trillions of microbes live in your gut, almost all of them in the colon (large intestine). Stomach acid, the gut’s sweeping waves and your immune system keep bacteria in the small intestine few and yeast in check. When those defenses slip, bacteria crowd the small intestine (SIBO) or Candida yeast multiplies. Parasites are caught from water, food or people.

Lifestyle RecommendationsCut the sugar, let your gut sweep, and keep new germs out

Cut the sugar

Skip added sugar, sweet drinks, alcohol and junk food during the plan.

Space your meals

No grazing: the gut sweeps itself between meals. Stop eating 2 hours before bed.

Wash, cook, filter

Wash hands and produce, cook meat through, and boil or filter lake water.

Eat calm, chew well

Slow, unhurried meals give stomach acid and enzymes a head start.

Review acid blockers

They lower stomach acid, a key defense. Never stop one without your prescriber.

Provider checkKeep taking your medicines.

  • On any medicine or high blood pressure? Berberine changes how the liver clears many medicines, such as cyclosporine, and can push blood sugar too low with diabetes drugs; Spectrum BR’s licorice can raise blood pressure. Bring your list to your provider.
  • On a blood thinner? Spectrum BR, Nutricidin, Paradex and Binder Pro can affect it. Ask your provider first.
  • Pregnant or nursing? Do not take Spectrum BR or Paradex; ask your provider about the rest. For a child? These are adult doses: ask first.
  • Central IV line or weak immunity? Skip S. boulardii unless your provider says yes. Tree-nut allergy or a seizure disorder? Ask before Paradex.
