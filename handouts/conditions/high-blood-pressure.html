High Blood Pressure
No symptoms, real risk

Nearly half of U.S. adults have high blood pressure, and many do not know it, because it rarely causes symptoms. Over the years, the extra force strains the heart, kidneys, brain and eyes and raises the risk of heart attack and stroke. One lever you control is nitric oxide (NO), the signal that tells blood-vessel walls to relax. What you eat, how you move, how you sleep and how you handle stress all change how much of it your body makes.

Lifestyle Recommendations

Eat for nitric oxide

Beets, arugula, spinach and celery bring nitrate. Load up on potassium-rich produce.

Cut the sodium

Aim under 1,500 to 2,300 mg a day. Most hides in packaged and restaurant food.

Move, then hold

Walk daily and add isometric holds (wall sits, hand grips) 3 times a week.

Sleep and breathe

Sleep 7 to 9 hours. Loud snoring? Ask about sleep apnea.

Quit smoking

Each cigarette tightens vessels. Keep alcohol low, too.

Skip harsh mouthwash

Antiseptic rinses wipe out the mouth bacteria that help make NO.
