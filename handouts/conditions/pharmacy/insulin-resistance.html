Understanding Insulin Resistance

Insulin is a hormone your pancreas releases, especially after you eat. It tells your muscle and fat cells to open their glucose doors and your liver to store sugar (glucose), so it leaves your blood and fuels your body. With insulin resistance, the cells stop listening well. Sugar starts to back up, so the pancreas pushes out more and more insulin to keep blood sugar normal. This can go on quietly for years before blood sugar climbs high enough to be called prediabetes or type 2 diabetes.

What drives it

  • Sugary drinks and processed food
  • Sitting most of the day
  • Short sleep, long-term stress
  • Extra fat around the belly
  • Family history, age 45 or older
  • Some medicines, such as steroids

Clues to watch for

  • Often no signs at all
  • Blood sugar creeping up on labs
  • High fasting insulin on a lab test
  • Dark skin at the neck or armpits
  • Skin tags in those same spots
  • High triglycerides, low good HDL

Where it can lead

  • Prediabetes, then type 2 diabetes
  • Heart disease and stroke
  • High blood pressure
  • Fatty liver disease
  • PCOS and hormone shifts
  • Memory and thinking problems
More than1 in 3

U.S. adults have prediabetes, and more than 8 in 10 of them do not know it (CDC). Insulin often climbs years before blood sugar does, so ask your doctor about a fasting insulin test along with the numbers below.

Pharmacist checkKeep taking your medicines.

  • Both have dihydroberberine. Gluco IR and Berberine Pro give 400 mg a day together: avoid both if pregnant or breastfeeding. It can slow the enzymes (CYP3A4, 2D6, 2C9) that clear drugs such as warfarin and cyclosporine. Fish oil and fenugreek can add to bleeding on a blood thinner: bring your full list.
  • Insulin or a sulfonylurea (such as glipizide)? Ask your prescriber before you start, and watch for low blood sugar. Metformin can lower B12 over time: ask for a check.
  • Thyroid medicine? Take it several hours apart from Gluco IR (with a morning thyroid pill, move Gluco IR to lunch and dinner): its chromium can lower how much you absorb. Gluco IR’s biotin can throw off some lab tests: tell the lab.
  • Weak immune system or on cyclosporine? Ask your pharmacist before Akkermansia Plus: it has live bacteria.
  • Allergic to fish, peanuts or chickpeas? Ask your pharmacist first: Omega Pure is fish oil, and the fenugreek in Gluco IR can trigger it too.
