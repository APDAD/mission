Joint Pain and Osteoarthritis
Knees, hips and hands

Osteoarthritis (OA), the most common arthritis, changes the whole joint: the smooth cartilage on the bone ends thins and cracks, bone spurs grow at the joint's edges, and the lining can swell. It hits knees, hips and hands most. About 595 million people had it in 2020, and the number is rising. Moving more and carrying less weight help most people stay active.

How it can feel
  • Pain with use (stairs, kneeling, opening jars) that eases with rest
  • Brief stiffness on waking or after sitting still
  • Creaking, grinding or clicking as it bends
  • Some swelling, or a joint that feels unsteady
  • Hard knobs on the finger joints
  • A sore hip often aches in the groin or buttock
Get checked promptly if
  • A hot, red, very swollen joint, above all with a fever: get care the same day
  • Morning stiffness over 30 minutes, or several swollen joints at once
  • It began with a fall or injury, or is worsening fast
  • It suddenly locks or gives way, or changes shape fast

Lifestyle Recommendations

Move most days

Moving feeds cartilage: walk, cycle or swim 150 minutes a week.

Build strength

Thigh and hip work 2 days a week takes load off the joint.

Lighten the load

Each pound lost takes about 4 pounds off your knees with every step.

Tai chi or yoga

Slow, steady moves ease stiffness and build balance.

Food for joints

Mediterranean style: fish, olive oil, beans, nuts, colorful plants.

Heat and support

Heat for stiffness, ice after a flare, a cane on your good side.

Pharmacist checkKeep taking your medicines.

  • On a blood thinner, or surgery coming up? Ask your pharmacist before Inflam-Eze Plus, Curcumin 400X or Chondro-Relief Intensive Care: you may need to pause them.
  • Inflam-Eze Plus has willow bark, a cousin of aspirin. Aspirin allergy? Skip it; Curcumin 400X is willow-free. On aspirin, ibuprofen or naproxen? Ask your pharmacist.
  • Liver or gallbladder trouble, or a stomach ulcer? Ask your pharmacist before Inflam-Eze Plus, Curcumin 400X or Chondro-Relief Intensive Care. Yellow skin or eyes, or dark urine? Stop them and call your doctor.
  • Shellfish allergy? Skip Chondro-Relief Intensive Care: it has green-lipped mussel.
  • Red-meat (alpha-gal) allergy? Skip Collagen Renew (beef). Chondro-Relief Intensive Care and Curcumin 400X may contain cow or pig gelatin or chondroitin: ask your pharmacist.
  • Pregnant or nursing? Skip Joint Eze and Inflam-Eze Plus (willow bark); ask your pharmacist about the rest. Already take a multivitamin? Chondro-Relief Intensive Care adds manganese: ask your pharmacist.
