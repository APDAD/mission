Mold Illness
Mycotoxin exposure and recovery

Mold grows wherever water lingers: a slow roof leak, a damp crawl space, an overflowing AC drip pan. Some molds make mycotoxins, natural toxins carried on spores and dust. Damp, moldy homes raise the chances of coughing, wheezing and asthma symptoms by about 30 to 50 percent, and many people also report tiredness, headaches and brain fog. The signs are broad, so mold is easy to miss. Recovery starts with removing the exposure.

Lifestyle Recommendations

Limit moldy foods

Peanuts, corn, long-stored grains, dried fruit, wine and aged cheese carry more mycotoxins.

More fiber

Fiber binds toxins carried in bile. Aim for 30 g a day.

Dry the air

Keep indoor humidity between 30 and 50 percent with a dehumidifier.

Filter it

HEPA air purifiers and HEPA vacuums trap mold spores.

Sweat

Sauna or a good workout helps you sweat. Drink water.

Fix fast

Dry or repair any leak within 24 to 48 hours.
