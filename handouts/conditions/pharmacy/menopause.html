Menopause and Perimenopause
Every system changes. Every system can be supported.

Menopause is the day you reach 12 months in a row with no period, on average around age 51. The years before it, perimenopause, often last 4 to 8 years while estrogen and progesterone swing, then fall. These hormones act on nearly every tissue, so changes show up head to toe. Hormone therapy works best for hot flashes: ask your doctor if it fits you.

Hot flashes: what works best

  1. Hormone therapy works best. For most healthy women under 60, the benefits outweigh the risks. It needs a prescription, so ask your doctor.
  2. Also proven: CBT (a brief talk therapy), clinical hypnosis and some prescriptions.
  3. Supplements are not proven for hot flashes. If you try one, score your symptoms again at 6 weeks to see whether it helps.
Source: The Menopause Society, 2022 and 2023.

Lifestyle Recommendations

Lift weights

Strength work 2 to 3 days a week guards muscle and bone.

Walk most days

150 minutes a week helps your heart, mood, sleep and weight.

Bone-smart plate

Protein at each meal and calcium foods like yogurt and greens.

Guard your sleep

Same bedtime, a cool dark room, less alcohol and late caffeine.

Try talk therapy

CBT (a talk therapy) and hypnosis are proven to ease hot flashes.

Skip smoking

Smoking speeds bone loss and raises heart risk.

Pharmacist checkKeep taking your medicines.

  • Pregnant, could be, or nursing? Ever had breast cancer or another estrogen-fed cancer? The labels say Menopause and PeriMenopause Support are not for you.
  • Liver disease, dark urine, or yellow skin or eyes? Stop Menopause or PeriMenopause Support and call your doctor: black cohosh and ashwagandha can rarely harm the liver.
  • Warfarin or another blood thinner? The label does not recommend D3 5000 with K2. Thyroid pills, or drugs that make you sleepy? Ashwagandha can add to them.
  • Vitamin D: test first, use short-term, recheck; 5,000 IU is over the 4,000 IU upper limit. Kidney disease? Ask your pharmacist before Mag Calm Pro or Creatine.
  • Doxycycline, ciprofloxacin or similar antibiotics? Take them 2 hours before or 4 to 6 hours after Mag Calm Pro. Keep alendronate (a bone drug) 2 hours apart, and thyroid pills 4 hours.
