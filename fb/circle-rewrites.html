For the family practices in the room. A 2025 meta-analysis in BMC Pediatrics, 17 randomized trials, 18,372 kids: daily low dose vitamin D, 1000 IU or less, was associated with a 58 percent lower risk of acute respiratory infection. Pooled across all dosing schedules the effect was not significant. The signal lived in daily, modest dosing. Daily beats big and occasional.

Right as sick season starts, that is a conversation worth having with every parent in your practice.

Full text: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12887-025-06361-6

Where we land in practice: test first, then daily modest dosing through sick season, and retest before spring. One number on the chart beats dosing by the calendar, and a parent who sees the number is the parent who keeps giving it every day.
