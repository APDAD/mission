The Winter Question,
Finally Ranked.

 

Hope you are well. Every October the same question walks into your clinic wearing different coats: what should my family actually take this winter? A Lancet-family journal just answered it the way we wish more papers would, by ranking everything at once.

The network meta-analysis pooled 107 trials and 101,751 adults, prevention only. Multi-strain probiotics cut infection incidence about 10 percent, shortened each infection by about a day with high certainty, and eased severity. For flu and COVID specifically, high-dose vitamin D was the standout. Nothing tested raised adverse events over placebo.

The modesty matters: a 10 percent reduction is real and it is not a force field, and the strains were pooled, so no single bottle owns these numbers. What the paper earns is a place for the gut in your winter-prevention conversation, next to the vitamin D you are probably already running.
