The Research

The Beta-Glucan
In Immunity Pro, Tested.

 

Hope you are well. For patients carrying steady work and family stress, here is a trial worth knowing. A trial published February 25, 2026 in iScience tested the same Wellmune beta-glucan that is in Immunity Pro: 198 moderately stressed adults, 12 weeks, 120 mg or 204 mg a day against placebo.

The mood result: tension, anger and total mood scores fell on both doses and rose on placebo (p < 0.001). The authors call mood a secondary, exploratory measure: not corrected for multiple tests, and modest in size. The picture below draws tension to scale. No safety concerns were seen.

Mood was not the trial's main measure. That was a self-reported respiratory questionnaire, and its result is in the paper. The trial ran in young adults (average age 28) at one university in Malaysia and was industry-supported.
