The Research

What Moved,
And What Did Not.

 

Hope you are well. The question we get most about Gluco IR is what the research on its lead ingredient actually shows. A review published July 16, 2025 in Frontiers in Pharmacology pooled twelve placebo-controlled trials of berberine, 889 adults with at least one metabolic syndrome marker.

Two-hour glucose, triglycerides, LDL, fasting glucose and waist all moved against placebo. Blood pressure and HDL did not, and adverse events were no different from placebo. The picture below draws each one to scale.
