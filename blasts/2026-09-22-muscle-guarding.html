Hope you are well. Rest is when muscle recovers, and plenty of patients on an adjusting schedule are not getting much of it. Muscle Eze is three capsules at bedtime: magnesium citrate, calcium lactate, L-theanine and passion flower. It helps relax muscle tissue and supports calmness.

The magnesium has a trial behind it. 22 college-age adults took 350 mg a day for ten days, and soreness after a hard bench press session came in one to two points lower on a six-point scale, with no change on placebo.
