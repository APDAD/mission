The Quiet Question
Under the Headline.

 

Hope you are well. You have probably seen the news: Thorne is being acquired by Procter and Gamble for 3.8 billion dollars, headed for the same portfolio as Metamucil, Align, and Vicks. It follows a long line. Pure Encapsulations and Douglas Labs went to Nestlé in 2017. Garden of Life the same year. Vital Proteins in 2020. Nature’s Bounty and Solgar in a 5.75 billion dollar purchase in 2021.

We are not here to tell you those are bad companies. We are here to name the question that comes with every one of these sales: when a practitioner brand starts answering to retail economics, who does it work for? You spent years lending these labels your credibility. When the same bottle shows up on a drugstore shelf and a subscription site, priced against your own dispensary, your recommendation keeps doing the marketing and someone else keeps the relationship.

So here is who we are, said plainly. NutriDyn has been family-owned for three generations and practitioner-exclusive since 1973. Our products are not on Amazon and not at retail, and the patients you send to us stay yours. Not because a contract says so this quarter, but because selling with you and never against you has been the whole business for fifty-three years.
