Hope you are well. The Thanksgiving Half is November 26 and the long-run blocks are loading Atlanta tendons right now, which makes this the right month for an honest collagen conversation.

A Stanford sports-medicine team reviewed 8 randomized trials of collagen peptides with resistance training. Tendon cross-section grew versus placebo in 3 of 4 trials that measured it, and tendon stiffness improved, but only in the trials dosing 15 to 30 grams a day. The 5-gram trials matched placebo. And on strength beyond training itself, the grade-A answer is no.

Two more things the paper says that a careful doctor will want: there are no pooled statistics, this is direction from 257 mostly male participants over short windows, and every winning protocol paired the collagen with vitamin C and heavy loading. The scoop without the gym did not build anything.
