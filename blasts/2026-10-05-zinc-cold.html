The Cochrane Read

Zinc Treats.
It Does Not Prevent.

 

Hope you are well. Georgia's flu surveillance season opens in October, and the zinc question comes back to every front desk with it.

The 2024 Cochrane review pooled 34 randomized trials, 8,526 children and adults, and split the question the way patients never do. For preventing colds, zinc did essentially nothing. For treating one already started, it cut duration by about 2.4 days, with low certainty and a wide range, and it bought that with more bad taste and nausea at the doses the trials used, 45 to 276 mg a day in short lozenge courses.

That split is the whole conversation. A daily 20 mg capsule like ours is a zinc-adequacy tool, not a treatment protocol, and we would rather say that out loud than borrow the trial numbers for it. The protocol question, which form and how much at first symptoms, belongs to you; the handout gives your patients the honest version.
