Everyone Moved a Little.
The Deficient Ones Moved.

 

Hope you are well. Quick one worth two minutes.

A new meta-analysis pooled 45 randomized trials on vitamin D and cardiometabolic markers. Across all of them the movement was modest. LDL down about 5 mg/dL, systolic pressure down about 2.8 mmHg, fasting glucose down about 2 mg/dL, A1c down about 0.16 percent.

Here is the part we would want you to hear from us. The glucose and A1c benefits showed up in patients who started below 20 ng/mL. The ones who already had a decent level barely moved, and the authors say plainly that the clinical significance is still uncertain.

are not the same conversation.
 

So if you are pulling a level before you recommend, this is the paper that backs how you already practice. And if you are not, it is a reasonable argument for starting.

Read the full study here: the vitamin D meta-analysis in the Asia Pacific Journal of Clinical Nutrition. Open access, no paywall.
