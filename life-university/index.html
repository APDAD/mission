The catch

Both are dormant, not merely quiet. Pulling every approved upcoming event on campus turned up zero scheduled meetings for either one through December 18. You cannot take a speaking slot at a club that has no slots.

So the ask is not "book a lunch-and-learn," it is "help them restart" — a slower and better conversation, routed through their faculty advisors and Student Engagement. Still the least contested ground on campus, just not the quick win it first looked like.

Why this account is winnable

The DC degree requires exactly two nutrition courses. Six credit hours. Students finish all of it by quarter 5 and do not touch a patient until quarter 8.

PUBH 8500 (Basic Nutrition, quarter 3) and PUBH 8505 (Nutrition and Health, quarter 5). That is the entire required nutrition curriculum across a fourteen-quarter program. There is a three-quarter hole between the last nutrition class a student takes and the first patient they counsel, and nobody fills it.

The reframe

NutriDyn's product at Life University is not supplements. It is the missing stretch between the classroom and the clinic floor. Fill that gap with genuinely good teaching and the brand comes along for free, for a whole career. That is also the only approach the administration will tolerate, since supplement brands are not permitted to be named inside the curriculum.

The four plays, ranked

1 · Nutrition CE through Life U

This is the biggest one and it has a clock on it.

Life University will apply for state CE approval on a sponsor's behalf — their own words: "Are you interested in having Life University apply for continuing education for your seminar? Email us at Postgrad@life.edu."

Because they are CCE-accredited, they hold automatic blanket CE-provider approval in Georgia. A course routed through them inherits it. Georgia lists nutrition as a qualifying clinical-science topic.

Two facts that make this urgent

There is zero nutrition CE on Life U's entire 2026–27 calendar. And every Georgia DC license expires December 31, 2026, with 40 hours required per renewal. Real deadline, real gap, and you already have the relationship.

This is no longer theoretical — it has been run once, on their campus

The doctor seminar was booked through Postgraduate Education — the exact department that applies for CE on a sponsor's behalf — and on Saturday Aug 29 it actually ran, four contact hours, in their own building, with students in the room. Their registration coordinator answered the original request in eight minutes, their director of postgraduate education was on the thread throughout, and the room fee is settled. The CE question now rides on a reply that was already owed, not a cold approach to a department nobody has met, and not a hypothetical event. A doctor seminar is worth one room. A CE-accredited series is worth a recurring line on Life U's own postgraduate calendar — same room, same work, ten times a year. A practicing doctor asked about CE credit for that seminar unprompted, before it happened, which is the market answering the question for itself.

2 · Own the clubs

AK Club is the beachhead and is already moving. Functional Health and Functional Nutrition are the follow-ons. The format proves itself once, then repeats.

The administrative key: Life U maintains a pre-approved guest speaker list, refreshed at least annually, split into technique and non-technique clubs. An outside presenter has to be on it. The process is not published anywhere. Getting on that list converts one-off visits into standing access and is the highest leverage piece of paperwork on this whole account.

Things you should know

Enrollment is falling hard

New DC enrollment is down roughly 57% from its 2021–22 peak: fall intake went from 222 to 135. Degrees conferred dipped to 304 in 2023–24 then rebounded to 417 in 2024–25, but conferrals lag intake by about four and a half years, so the intake decline has not landed yet.

Why it matters to you: a school under real enrollment pressure values a funding partner far more than one that is full. This is the best window you will get to ask for something structural.

Leadership is in flux

Robert Scott has resigned as president; Brian McAulay is interim. Re-confirm any title before you write to someone senior, because the org chart on file is from the Fall 2024 FactBook.

Competitors are already on campus

The Wellness Way, MaxLiving, AlignLife, Integrity Doctors and 100% Chiropractic all run student clubs at Life U.

They are selling practice models, not nutrition, so they are not head-on competition — but they are competing for the same student attention and they got there first.
