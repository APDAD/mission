The premise

A Peptide Is a Command

It tells the body to run a biochemical sequence. That sequence cannot run without raw material. Most of what gets charted as a side effect is a nutrient deficiency wearing a costume.

Her fatigue is ferritin, magnesium and B12. Her hair loss is protein collapse and zinc. Her flat mood is the B6 to serotonin to dopamine chain running dry. None of that is a medication failure, and all of it is inside what you are licensed to correct.

Why this year

The Ground Is Moving

  • Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are gone The shortage ended and the window closed. Patients who paid a couple hundred a month now face brand pricing or quitting, and a lot of them are quitting.
  • The federal picture is shifting again In July an FDA advisory committee voted to recommend several peptides for compounding, BPC-157 among them. That vote is advisory and nothing has changed in law yet, but the direction is unmistakable.
  • Georgia is enforcing The state board has been running unannounced inspections and disclosed hundreds of open investigations in June. Knowing exactly where your scope begins and ends has never mattered more than it does this year.

This seminar sits on the right side of that line by design. It is not about prescribing anything. It is about the nutritional protocol that decides whether a patient thrives on a peptide or abandons it.
